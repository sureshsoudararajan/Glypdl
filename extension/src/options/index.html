Privacy & Local Operation
🛡️ 100% Local & Private: Glypdl never collects browsing history, passwords, or personal metrics.
🍪 Zero Cookie Transmission: Browser cookies are never extracted or transmitted by this extension. For authenticated media, Glypdl's local desktop application handles authentication directly on your computer.
🔒 No DRM Circumvention: Protected media is detected and flagged without attempting decryption.
