Glypdl is a fast, modern, and privacy-focused open-source media downloader for Android, powered by native yt-dlp and FFmpeg.

Key Features:
• Download videos and audio from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and thousands of supported websites.
• Seamless adaptive video and audio stream merging via integrated native FFmpeg.
• In-app browser cookie management and session authentication for protected media.
• Format selection with audio-only (M4A/MP3) and high-resolution video options.
• Background download manager with pause, resume, queue, and persistent notifications.
• Persistent download history and Storage Access Framework (SAF) integration.
• 100% Free and Open Source (GPL-3.0-or-later) with zero advertisements, zero tracking, and complete privacy.
